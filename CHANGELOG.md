Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH:

- **PATCH**: bug fixes only;
- **MINOR**: new features or options;
- **MAJOR**: big changes, e.g. ones that need setting the addon up again.

## 0.18.0

- Proc alerts: Elemental Focus for shamans (the talent, 16164; its proc is the buff named Clearcasting, 16246), as SpellActivationOverlay has it in Forever: the art of a mage's Clearcasting (`GENERIC_ARC_05`) on both sides, at 150%, moving as the animation picked, like every proc. ## 0.17.1

- Fixed: missing buffs, at a boss pull the buffs the game may hide (seen with a mage's armor and Arcane Intellect, in a dungeon) showed as missing for about 2 seconds, then went. The game hides them from addons not only in the character's own fight but under any of its addon restrictions (`C_RestrictedActions`: Combat, Encounter, ChallengeMode, PvPMatch, Map), and a boss encounter starts at the pull, before the character is in combat itself: the read left them out and they were taken as gone until the character's own fight began. Those restrictions now count as a fight: the buffs stay as they were, and their own icons, moved by the game, take over. `ADDON_RESTRICTION_STATE_CHANGED` is listened to (the game sends it before a restriction is enforced, "Activating", while the auras can still be read; its state is kept, as `IsAddOnRestrictionActive` answers false while it's being sent), and the game is asked too (a restriction already on after a /reload). The chat's restriction doesn't count. `/ke buffscan` says which restrictions are on.
- Tests: a boss pull before the character's own fight (the Encounter restriction, "Activating", then on): the buff's own icon, unseen while the buff is on and seen when it goes; the usual reads back when it ends; a restriction on with no word from the game, asked for; the chat's restriction not counting; `/ke buffscan` naming the restriction.

## 0.17.0

- General: "Hide the quest tracker" (off by default), under a new "Quests" header: the list of the quests followed, on the right of the screen (the game's objective tracker, "All Objectives"), stays hidden while it's on (`Modules\QuestTracker.lua`, a new file: the game must be restarted after this update). Turned off, the list comes back if the game was showing it (in a fight, when the fight ends); while off, it's left alone, for other addons.
- Chat: `/ke errors clear` (or `/ke clearerrors`, or `/ke clear errors`: the same three ways in the three Kyoryokuna addons) empties the list of errors kept (the last 30, never emptied before, so errors of old versions stayed in `/ke errors` for good). It's in `/ke help`, and the chat says so again at the next error. Before, "errors clear" was an unknown command, which opens the options.
- Tests: `/ke errors clear`, `/ke clearerrors` and `/ke clear errors` empty the list (not opening the options), and the next error is kept again.
- Tests: a new test covers the quest tracker (each character's own, from the page too, not in a code; left alone while off; hidden when turned on; hidden again each time the game shows it; in a fight only made invisible, then hidden when the fight ends; back when turned off, only if the game was showing it; turned off in a fight, back when the fight ends).

## 0.16.0

- Missing buffs: each group (Essential, Utility) has an animation and a glow of its own, in its look. "Animation" as the proc alerts': None (default), Pulse, Breathe, Blink or Float, with "Speed (one cycle)" (0.5 to 3 seconds) and "Strength" (2% to 20%). - How it's done: each icon is now its place in the group (with the group's opacity) and, inside it, what's seen, which moves (so a fade never fights the opacity). A group's icons move in step: when one shows, or the settings change, all of the group's start again; a read that changes nothing never starts them again. One clock moves every moving outline shown, in step, and stops when there's none. The glow, the outline and the border are textures of the icon itself (none of the game's frames is touched), so a buff the game hides in combat glows too: its own icon now sits in a frame inside its window that clips what's out of it, larger than the window by the glow's reach (a fifth of the icon's size, and 2 pixels), and it's that frame that moves; the game's container still starts where the window does, so the icon is seen or pushed far away as before, its glow with it. Those windows are no longer hidden and shown again at each read, only when no longer in use (hiding a frame may stop its animation). While a group pulses or floats, its icons' textures aren't fitted to the screen's pixels (`SetSnapToPixelGrid(false)`, `SetTexelSnappingBias(0)`, as the game does for its own health bars): fitted, an icon this small grew in jumps of a whole pixel, not smoothly (seen in game). Still or only fading, they're fitted again, sharp.
- The animations and the game's glow art are now shared by the proc alerts and the missing buffs (`Core\Effects.lua`, a new file: the game must be restarted after this update).
- Tests: a new test covers each group's animation and glow (nothing by default; Pulse on one group only, on each icon's inner part; not started again at each read, all started again together when a new icon shows; its textures not fitted to the pixels while it pulses or floats, fitted while still or fading; Breathe, Blink, Float, a new speed, the limits; the game's glow around the icon in gold, then red; the moving outline, its size, its clock moving every outline in step and stopping without one; both; the border and its size; nothing moving when turned off; the page's settings for each group). The test of a buff the game hides in combat checks its glow is never cut by its window, it moves with its group on the frame inside its window, and in combat its window is never hidden and shown again nor its animation started again.

## 0.15.1

- Fixed: missing buffs, a buff the game hides from addons in combat (a shaman's Lightning Shield) only showed as missing after the fight when it went in one. In combat WoW Forever leaves such an aura out of what addons read (no error, no secret value), so "not there" can't be believed for it; since the first version the addon kept what it knew when the list came empty, and so never saw it go.
- Now the game itself moves those buffs' icons, without the addon ever knowing. For each buff watched that the game says it may hide (C_Secrets: contextually or always secret), the addon makes one of the game's own AuraContainers (out of combat, once; given the new spells when a new rank is learned), never seen (no alpha), with one group for that buff alone (any rank known, or the family's group versions; one frame at most, given a width of 1000). The game sizes the container by what's in it: 1 wide while the buff is gone, 1000 wide while it's on. The game's own code says addons may tie their frames to such a container if they're made with `DisableUntrustedLayoutScriptsTemplate` (their layout scripts never run, so they can't learn its size). The buff's own icon is such a frame (only textures and text in it), tied to the container's right side, inside a "window" of its group (a frame that clips what's out of it), where the container starts too: while the buff is on, the container pushes the icon 1000 points out of its window (unseen); when it goes, the icon sits in its window and is seen at once. Nothing is read, and the game's frames are never touched (in combat the game forbids addons to touch them or anything in them: "Attempt to access forbidden object", seen in game with /ke buffscan). In combat the window takes the buff's place in its group, after the others, at the group's size; no click on it (it's there unseen while the buff is on); out of combat the group's usual icons are back. Without a container (it can't be made or set up), the buff stays in combat as it was when the fight began. The buffs the game never hides are read as always, in combat too: an empty list of auras is no buff at all.
- A first try didn't work in game and is gone: a mask (MaskTexture) put in a frame of the game's own (an aura slot, shown by the game only while the buff is on) and given to the buff's own icon. No false icon, but none when the buff went either: a hidden mask seems to keep cutting. Out of combat the game already answers "secret" for whether its slot is shown.
- Missing buffs: when the game refuses some auras in combat (an error, or a secret value while reading them all), each buff watched is asked for by its name (C_UnitAuras.GetAuraDataBySpellName): the ones the game still gives follow at once.
- /ke buffscan: what each buff watched answers by its name and how secret the game says its aura is, whether the game's AuraContainers were made, and for each buff the game may hide whether its own icon is ready (or why not), with the container's width and how far the icon is from the start of its window (or "secret"); the whole scan is kept in the saved data (read from the file after a /reload), like /ke procscan.
- /ke bufftest (out of combat): the own icons of the buffs the game may hide shown for 10 seconds in their group as in a fight, under "KE test" (seen with the buff gone, unseen with it on), and the container's width and where the icon is then, kept in the saved data.
- Tests: a buff the game hides in combat (left out of what's read): a container for each such buff, never seen, with a group for that buff alone (one frame, wide), the buff's own icon made with the game's template in a window of its group that clips, tied to the container's right side, the container at the window's start; a container that can't be set up put away, and said by /ke buffscan; a new rank given to the same container; in combat not taken as gone, its own icon in place at the group's size (after the buffs read as always, the group's icon under it gone), unseen while the game widens the container, seen as soon as it shrinks; no click on it; without a container, kept as it was; put away after the fight and when turned off; /ke bufftest. In combat, a buff the game never hides is read as always (the last one gone shows at once); refused auras asked for by name.

## 0.11.1

- Trainer: the trainer's list is read when the game sends it, not at the start of the visit. In WoW Forever the list is empty at TRAINER_SHOW and a change of the trainer's filters only shows with the next TRAINER_UPDATE (Blizzard_TrainerUI waits for it too: filterPending), so the whole list was never read at the start: the class's list stayed empty, and the character's own was only filled by the updates that followed, with the trainer's filters as the player had them. Now the "Available", "Unavailable" and "Used" filters that are off are turned on at the start of the visit and the list is read each time it comes (TRAINER_UPDATE): the first reading of a visit starts the lists again, the next ones add to them. The filters are put back as they were as soon as the list has come with their spells (half a second after the first reading, after one more, when it has none of that kind; also when the trainer is closed before). The "Used" filter is only turned on while the class's list doesn't have the learned spells yet: once it has read them ("complete"), it's never turned on again and the class's list is added to at each visit, not started again (the spells learned later were on the trainer's list before), so the learned spells don't flash on the trainer's window. The last visit's list is no longer wiped before the new one has come. The list is read again once its names and ranks have loaded (TRAINER_SERVICE_INFO_NAME_UPDATE, one event per line: a single reading 0.3 s after the last).
- Trainer: a line read before the game has its rank's text came without a rank: kept as another spell ("Mage Armor" beside "Mage Armor Rank 3", same spell), so it showed twice, and the first rank of the spell seemed taught by the trainer: "Pyroblast" without a rank made Pyroblast's ranks show again for a frost mage. Now the rank comes from the spell itself when the line has none (C_Spell.GetSpellSubtext), a line without a rank beside ranks of the same spell is left out (when read, and when shown, for the lists kept before), and such a line never counts as a first rank.
- Trainer: the trainer gives no level for a spell already learned (0): it's now the level the game says the spell is learned at (C_Spell.GetSpellLevelLearned), or the one kept before; shown at level 1 if still unknown.
- Trainer: every trainer that opens the class trainer's window counts, each with its own spells. In WoW Forever the weapon masters, the riding trainers and the mage's portal trainer open it too, and the game calls them class trainers: a visit to one of them replaced the whole list with its few spells (only "Portal: Ironforge" after the portal trainer). Each kept spell now says its trainer ("from", the trainer's name) and the group the trainer's window gives it ("category", the 6th value of GetTrainerServiceInfo); a visit starts only that trainer's spells again, and the lists shown put every trainer's together: the character's own spells from the trainers it has been to, and its class's from the others. The character's trainers (trainerVisits: name -> place) and the class's (visits: name -> place and who went) replace the single last visit, and the class's list is "complete" per trainer. The spellbook's tab names the trainer with the most spells, then "And also:" the others. The lists kept before are given to the trainer of their last visit.
- Trainer: a rank needing the rank before it no longer keeps that as a requirement when the readings after the filters went back no longer show the first rank: the spells taught from their first rank are those on the list now and those kept for the class during the visit. When shown, a requirement the trainer teaches from its first rank never hides a spell (for the lists kept before).
